What I'm usually hired for

Most Unity freelancers stick to one lane: mobile, or XR, or tooling. I've worked across all three, which helps when a project runs into something unfamiliar. Here's a rough breakdown of what I do.

  • VR / AR training simulations Built for Aptara and independent clients: a Navy VR safety training app, forklift and excavator handling sims (Oculus Rift + Logitech G29), a WebGL fire-extinguisher training simulation, and an AR app for training field technicians on fiber network equipment.
  • Mobile games, shipped and live At Godspeed Games: Snakes & Ladders 3D (multiplayer via Photon, 10M+ downloads), Godspeed Cricket League (FSM-based AI, mocap animation, 1M+ downloads), Match Harmony (Skillz SDK, iOS port), and a few kids' coloring/drawing games with custom shader-based systems, also 1M+ downloads.
  • Tools and shaders that outlast a single project A custom xNode-based Behavior Tree editor, a reflection-based in-game debug console used across multiple titles, and custom shaders for water, snow, and sprite-coloring effects. The kind of thing a studio keeps reusing long after the original project ships.
