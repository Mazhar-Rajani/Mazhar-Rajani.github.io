What I'm usually hired for

Most Unity freelancers focus on one area: mobile, XR, or tooling. I've worked across all three, which helps when a project runs into something unfamiliar. Here's a rough breakdown of what I do.

  • VR / AR training simulations Built in studio roles and for independent clients: a Navy VR safety training app, forklift and excavator handling sims driven by a real steering wheel and pedals, a fire-extinguisher training sim that runs in the browser, and an AR app for training field technicians on fiber network equipment.
  • Mobile games, shipped and live At Godspeed Games: Snakes & Ladders 3D (real-time multiplayer, 10M+ downloads), Godspeed Cricket League (computer-controlled players and motion-captured animation, 1M+ downloads), Match Harmony (a competitive tournament puzzle game, including the iOS version), and a few kids' coloring and drawing games with a custom coloring system, also 1M+ downloads.
  • Tools and effects that get reused across projects A visual editor for game AI, an in-game debug console used across multiple titles, and custom visual effects for water, snow and coloring. The kind of thing a studio keeps reusing long after the original project ships.
