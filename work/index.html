How projects work

  • One contact You talk to Mazhar from the first call to handover. We reply fast, give realistic timelines, and tell you early if something is going to slip.
  • The right people for the job Each project gets only the people it needs, from development and art to QA and project management. Nobody is billed to a project they are not working on.
  • One contract, one invoice You sign one contract and get one invoice, however many people work on the project, and we're accountable for the whole delivery.
  • How the work is priced Short projects are fixed-price, quoted once the scope is clear. Longer ones run as a monthly retainer per person, and day rates cover small tasks and add-on work. Ask for the rate card and we'll send it.
  • Terms Fixed-price work is 40% upfront and the rest on milestones; retainers are billed at the start of the month. Two revision rounds per milestone. Source code and IP transfer to you on final payment. We sign NDAs before you share anything sensitive.
