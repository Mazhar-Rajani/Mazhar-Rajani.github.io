About Me

Hi! I'm Mazhar, a game programmer from India previously employed with Godspeed Games as a Senior Unity Developer. Playing games has always been a passion of mine, and my curiosity about "how these games are developed?" led me to the field of game creation, where I learned about numerous aspects of game design and production.

Here you can see some of the projects to which I've been able to contribute, as well as some of the personal projects that I've been working on. When I'm not programming, I like to learn new things, take on new challenges, and spend extra time refining my skills to ensure that the creative vision is realized to the best of my ability, or I spend time with my family.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if you have any questions :)
